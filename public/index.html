Discover LYMNOUS

Keeping minds active with dignity and joy

With over 55 million people worldwide experiencing cognitive decline—and cases projected to reach 139 million by 2050 as the global population ages—memory preservation is more critical than ever. LYMNOUS integrates interactive stimulation games, daily reflection journals, and secure memory albums to support independent cognitive health with dignity and peace of mind.

We set out to create a solution that is encouraging, intuitive, and enjoyable to use. Today, LYMNOUS combines cognitive exercises, memory tools, and features that encourage social interaction and meaningful daily activities.

Our Story

A family passion project inspired by the journey of a loved one.

Preserving Dignity

My father, a former International Director of Sales for a multinational company, was reluctant to engage in the activities suggested by my mother and his guests. Given his background and accomplishments, the exercises felt childish, impersonal, and even humiliating.

The Inspiration

LYMNOUS was inspired by my father’s journey through cognitive decline. As I stood by him, I witnessed not only the struggle of gradually losing parts of himself, but also the lack of dignified tools and exercises available to help delay its progression.

The Conception

That experience led me to create LYMNOUS — a platform designed to preserve identity, dignity, and connection through meaningful exercises rooted in the individual’s own life, experiences, routines, and personal history.

A Spring of Hope

Our Mission

Slowing cognitive decline and preserving identity through positive mental activity.

Scientific research suggests that cognitive stimulation and meaningful mental engagement may help individuals experiencing cognitive impairment maintain their abilities, remain socially connected, and preserve aspects of their independence and personal identity for a longer period of time.

While mental exercises cannot cure the underlying neurodegenerative disease, they may help slow cognitive decline, support daily functioning, encourage social interaction, and improve overall quality of life.

LYMNOUS's mission is to provide personalized cognitive exercises and meaningful interactions inspired by each individual's life experiences, memories, interests, family relationships, and personal history.

LYMNOUS Q&A

Answers to frequently asked questions regarding administration and usage.

Q1: Who should set up the LYMNOUS account?

The account should be set up by the main user whenever possible, or by the nearest family member living in the household. To ensure the highest level of privacy and security, we do not encourage external guests or helpers to open the account. Furthermore, the account must be created directly on the user's personal computer to establish the correct secure login environment.

Q2: Why is setting up my profile important?

The User Profile drives the entire LYMNOUS experience, so it is absolutely essential that all information provided is true, accurate, and honest. All AI interactions, games, and memories are dynamically generated based on this specific information, so taking the time to set it up truthfully ensures a deeply personalized and rewarding experience.

Q3: What is the correct sequence of inputs for setup?

It is crucial to carefully follow the "Input & Setup" steps in order: first enter the Basic Information, then manage the Photo Album, and finally invite Guests and Family Members.

Q4: How do I manage the album?

Managing the album may be done gradually; however, the more photos uploaded, the richer the experience becomes. You can invite up to 10 friends and family members to contribute photos with narrations to build a vibrant memory collection.

Q5: Can other family members participate?

Yes! The primary account manager can invite family members as Guests or Friends & Family via secure invitation links. Once registered, Guests can log in using their credentials to play cognitive stimulation games side-by-side with the user, while Friends & Family can upload photos and stories.

Q6: How does the Daily Journal work?

The journal provides gentle prompts to encourage daily reflection, social connection, and healthy habits. We strongly encourage making daily notes or having a loved one assist. This daily routine stimulates cognitive recall and tracks personal interests over time. Your journal history is securely stored for 30 days.

Q7: Are the brain games too difficult?

No. Our adaptive AI seamlessly tailors the difficulty, vocabulary, and themes to match the user's specific age, background, hobbies, and life history so that exercises always feel rewarding and enjoyable rather than frustrating.

Q8: How is progress tracked?

The primary caregiver has access to a comprehensive Wellness Report that summarizes engagement, participation trends, game completion rates, and cognitive activity patterns over time.

Q9: What is the specific role of the Admin?

The Admin (Primary Caregiver) sets up and configures the system on behalf of the Main User (Patient). This includes initializing their profile parameters (career, favorite music, hobbies) to fuel AI personalization, uploading initial photos, and inviting other guests to play side-by-side using the interactive 2-Player turn tracker.

Q10: How do we play games together side-by-side?

On any game page, select "Play Together (2 Players)" on the game mode card. This displays the Pass & Play turn tracker bar at the top. The active player alternates automatically after each turn, making it easy for two people to collaborate on a single tablet or computer.

Q11: What is the difference between the Admin and a Guest?

The Admin manages patient profiles, configures call connection buttons, invites new members, and views Wellness/Usage Reports. Guests (invited friends and helpers) log in to play cognitive games with the patient or upload photos, but do not have access to administrative settings or private logs.

Q12: How can the Main User log in independently?

The Main User (Patient) can play games independently by logging in via Kiosk Mode. Selecting the Kiosk portal and entering their dedicated 4-digit PIN code launches their simplified activities dashboard instantly without requiring passwords.

Q13: How do the subscription plans work?

LYMNOUS offers three flexible subscription tiers: Free, Premium ($19/mo), and Gold ($29/mo). Higher tiers unlock expanded photo storage, unlimited guest links, and advanced AI features—including personalized AI Story Builder dictation, dynamic game difficulty tuning, custom trivia, and full wellness analytics.

Q14: What scientific research and clinical references support LYMNOUS?

LYMNOUS is built upon evidence-based findings in neurocognitive rehabilitation and dementia care:

• The Lancet Commission on Dementia Prevention, Intervention, and Care (2020 & 2024 Updates): Concluded that frequent cognitive engagement, social interaction, and mental stimulation build cognitive reserve and support everyday functional independence.

• Cognitive Stimulation Therapy (CST) Guidelines (World Health Organization / NICE): Group and one-on-one structured mental exercises (word association, categorizing, reminiscence, memory puzzles) produce significant benefits in cognitive function and quality of life.

• Autobiographical Reminiscence Research: Studies published in the Journal of Alzheimer's Disease demonstrate that personal photos paired with narrated memories activate deep episodic pathways, reducing anxiety and strengthening identity preservation.

Q15: How does LYMNOUS protect user privacy and personal memories?

All family photos, journal reflections, and personal profile information are strictly encrypted and confidential. We never sell, track, or share your data with third parties or advertisers. Access is restricted exclusively to the authorized caregiver, patient PIN, and explicitly invited family members.
